Philadelphia Basketball Development

Philadelphia Basketball Training That Measures Progress and Builds Game-Ready Players

NBG helps elementary, middle school, and high school athletes improve through structured, age-appropriate training with clear development goals, individual feedback, and regular progress tracking.

Players do more than complete drills. They learn how to apply skills in real game situations: making reads, creating space, finishing through pressure, defending, and competing with confidence.

Why NBG

A Measurable Development System, Not Random Drills

NBG identifies what each athlete needs to improve, trains those areas with purpose, and reassesses progress over time. Every player works toward clear development priorities based on age, experience, position, and current ability.

01

Individual Development Goals

Players receive specific priorities such as weak-hand control, shooting consistency, finishing, defense, or decision-making.

02

Game-Transfer Training

Training includes reads, reactions, spacing, defensive pressure, live competition, and decision-making—not only stationary repetitions.

03

Measurable Progress

Development is tracked through evaluations, coach feedback, skill benchmarks, and periodic reassessment.

04

Age + Skill-Based Instruction

Athletes train in groups designed for their stage of development so sessions are challenging without being mismatched.

05

Player Understanding

Players learn why a skill matters, when to use it, and how it connects to team basketball.

06

Consistent Feedback

Players receive corrections during training and clear direction about what to continue developing between sessions.

The NBG Process

A Structured Development System

1

Evaluate

Identify strengths, weaknesses, habits, and development priorities.

2

Set Goals

Establish specific areas for improvement based on age, skill level, and competitive goals.

3

Develop

Build skills through purposeful, game-based training.

4

Apply

Use those skills in decision-making drills, pressure situations, competition, and live play.

5

Track

Record coach observations, skill benchmarks, and areas requiring continued attention.

6

Reassess

Review progress and adjust development priorities as the athlete improves.

About NBG

Developing Basketball Players and Young People

Northeast Bucket Getters is a Philadelphia-based basketball development organization helping young athletes grow on and off the court through skill development, basketball IQ, competition, discipline, confidence, accountability, and leadership.

NBG is led by Coach Ticoh Dallo, a youth-development professional with ten years of experience who currently serves as Athletic Director for the Bridesburg Boys & Girls Club in Philadelphia.

The objective is not simply to help athletes complete more drills. It is to help them understand the game, make better decisions, compete with confidence, and continue improving.
